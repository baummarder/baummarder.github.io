Who am I?

I started developing at the age of 14 in an IMP class at school with HTML and Processing. From there, I expanded into Python and Java, building small games, Minecraft mods, and various hardware and software experiments.

Project Status: Many of my projects (including several Minecraft mods) are currently private and in active development. Some of them might be released publicly later!
Fun Fact: I used BlueJ for Java, KompoZer for HTML, and IDLE for Python for a long time. Today I use VS Code and IntelliJ, but I still love BlueJ for its simplicity! Parts of my web projects were even built in KompoZer.
